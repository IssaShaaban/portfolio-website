What it is

Wyst is a social app for meeting people nearby. Rather than starting from a profile, it starts from what you like doing: you pick your interests, find small groups built around them, and turn up to local events with people who already have something in common with you.

The app is live on the App Store and Google Play, and is launching in Manchester first.

How it works

  1. Pick your interests

    Board games, cycling, photography. Your interests become the filters for everything you see.

  2. Join small groups

    Each group has its own chat, so you can get to know people before meeting them.

  3. Go to events

    Upcoming events sit alongside your groups, so plans turn into actually meeting up.

Smart Home System

Raspberry PiCC++React.jsSQL

The dashboard

A high-performance web interface that visualises live telemetry from a Raspberry Pi. Temperature, humidity and atmospheric pressure update in real time over a WebSocket pipeline, which keeps latency between the hardware sensors and the browser under a second.

The backend

main.cpp sets up three things: the ServerConnection, which uses the libwebsockets library to talk to the frontend; the Bme280Sensor, which reads from the GPIO pins on the Raspberry Pi; and a SQLite database.

The Request class follows a typical request design. It holds an id, the time of the request, a source and destination, and a request type (get, post, getAll or close). Each request arrives from the frontend as JSON and is converted into this class.

Future additions

  • Specific buttons for get, post, getAll and close connection
  • Extra sensors in different rooms
  • A centralised database on its own Raspberry Pi
  • A UI that displays several areas at once
  • Periodic reads from the sensors, and periodic writes to the database
